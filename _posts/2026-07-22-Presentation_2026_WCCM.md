On July 22, 2026, I presented our research at **WCCM-ECCOMAS 2026**, held from July 19 to 24, 2026, in Munich, Germany.

The presentation, titled **"Text-Guided Multiscale Topology Optimization for Mechanical Anisotropy Design with TPMS,"** introduced a text-guided multiscale topology optimization framework that translates natural-language design intent into macrostructural geometry and TPMS-based anisotropic material distributions.

The framework extends topology optimization beyond predefined numerical objectives by incorporating qualitative design intent related to geometry, directional stiffness, and programmed yielding behavior.
